I’m a Data Scientist with a Master’s degree in Data Science and Business Informatics from the University of Pisa, increasingly focused on AI engineering.

Over the past year, I’ve worked on a platform for multimodal tracking of smart containers. I’m responsible end-to-end for the ML services and several backend endpoints, taking models from design through to integration.

I’ve also taken part in several internal projects and GenAI competitions, notably alongside partners such as IBM and ElevenLabs.

I enjoy pushing AI to its limits to explore what’s possible at the bleeding edge, especially with AI agents. At the same time, I value ML and MLOps, and the system design behind reliable ML systems.
